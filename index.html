About

Engineer who builds what he tests

I'm an R&D engineer with an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Rutgers and hands-on medical device experience across design controls, test method development and validation, and GMP/GLP documentation under ISO 13485 and 21 CFR Part 820.

At Ethicon (Johnson & Johnson MedTech) I develop ASTM-based mechanical test methods on Instron systems and design the custom fixtures that make those tests possible, taking each one from concept sketch through CAD, prototype and validation.

I'm looking for a full-time R&D or design engineering role where I can keep doing both: designing the hardware and proving that it works.
